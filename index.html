Our Story

More Than Rice.
A Tradition of Quality.

Thirupati Rice Mill began as a small family milling unit on the outskirts of Karur, buying paddy directly from farmers we knew by name. Three decades later, that same habit hasn't changed — we still choose every lot by hand.

What has changed is how we process it. Today our mill combines the judgement of experienced grain selectors with modern de-stoning, precision milling and colour sorting — so the rice that reaches your kitchen is clean, uniform and consistently good, season after season.

  • Paddy sourced from trusted delta & Karur belt farms — selected for grain length, moisture and aroma before it enters the mill.
  • Modern machinery, traditional judgement — automated lines guided by millers who can read a handful of grain at a glance.
  • Retail packs and bulk supply — for homes, hotels, caterers and wholesale distributors across Tamil Nadu.

Why Thirupati

Why families and traders keep coming back

Good rice is not an accident. It is a series of small, disciplined decisions taken from the field to the final pack.

01

Carefully Selected Grains

Paddy is inspected lot by lot for moisture, grain length and broken percentage. Only what we would serve at our own table makes it into the mill.

02

Modern Milling

De-stoners, precision shellers, polishers and colour sorters work together to protect the grain — reducing breakage and lifting yield for every variety.

03

Hygienic Processing

Closed conveyors, dust extraction and stainless-steel contact surfaces keep the product clean from intake to packing. No stone, no dust, no compromise.

04

Consistent Quality

Retained samples from every batch let us match colour, aroma and cooking behaviour, order after order. The bag you buy next month tastes like this month's.

Our Process

From Paddy to Rice

Six deliberate stages stand between the field and your kitchen. Nothing is rushed, and nothing is skipped.

01

Paddy

Paddy arrives from partner farms, weighed, sampled and graded before it is accepted.

02

Cleaning

De-stoning and de-husking remove husk, straw, sand and immature grains.

03

Milling

Multi-stage shelling and polishing, set for each variety to reduce breakage.

04

Grading

Length graders and colour sorters separate whole grain from broken and discoloured.

05

Quality Check

Cooking tests, moisture checks and visual sampling before a batch is cleared.

06

Packing

Sealed, weighed and labelled — in retail packs or bulk bags, ready for dispatch.

Inside the Mill

Every stage happens under one roof.

Because intake, milling, grading and packing are all handled inside our Karur facility, we can trace a batch back to the day and the field it came from. It keeps quality tight — and it means your order leaves our gate the way it was milled.
